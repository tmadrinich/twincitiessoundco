01 / THE FOUNDER

Taylor Madrinich

FOUNDER · GUITAR · PRODUCTION

I've been on both sides of this. I play guitar in Whiskey Peddler, I produce records, and I've spent 10+ years leading sales, marketing, and operations teams. I studied music business at North Central University in Minneapolis.

When my brother, who performs as SAYWHEN, joined a touring band, I watched the process fall apart. The label fronted the money for the album and told them to go find a manager. Good luck. Nobody was working together.

Twin Cities Sound Co. is my answer to that. One team runs the label, recording, promotion, booking, and digital, so the people building an artist's career are actually on the same page.
