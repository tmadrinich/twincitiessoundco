02 / ABOUT

Run by people who've been on stage too

Twin Cities Sound Co. was started by working musicians who got tired of bad advance calls and worse representation. We help artists build the machine behind the music — and maintain a network of musicians, studios, and venues so you're never stuck figuring out who to call next.

Not every artist needs the same thing, and not every musician wants to be formally represented. Services are built around what you actually need, not a one-size-fits-all package.
