02 / ABOUT

Run by people who've been on stage too

Twin Cities Sound Company was started by working musicians and engineers who got tired of bad monitors and worse advance calls. We build every show the way we'd want it built if we were the ones walking out to play it.

We work directly with venues, promoters, and bands to keep load-in simple and the mix honest — whether that's a 200-cap room on a Tuesday or a two-day outdoor festival.
