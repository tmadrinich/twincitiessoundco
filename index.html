01 / WHY WE EXIST

Nobody was on the same page

When Taylor's brother, who performs as SAYWHEN, joined a touring band, the label fronted the money for the album and told them to go find a manager. Good luck. The booker didn't talk to the label. The label didn't talk to whoever ran the socials. Everyone did their piece, and nobody owned the whole thing.

Twin Cities Sound Co. exists to fix that. Your release, your shows, your promotion, and your online presence get planned together, by one team, so every piece pushes in the same direction.
